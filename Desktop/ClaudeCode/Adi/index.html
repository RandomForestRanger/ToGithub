🪵 Adi

A two-row mancala game of the Ewe people of Togo & Ghana

About Adi

Adi is played on an elaborately carved wooden board. Capture seeds from your opponent to grow your store across multiple rounds — until one player can no longer fill a row.

How to Play

  • You are Player 1 (bottom). The computer plays the top row.
  • Click any hole in your row with seeds to sow anti-clockwise — seeds spread right along your row, then left along the top row.
  • You cannot sow from the same hole twice in a row until the opponent sows into it.
  • Holes you cannot use show ✕.

Captures

If your last seed lands in an opponent's hole and makes the count exactly 3 → capture all 3!
Chain capture: If the hole just before the captured hole also has exactly 3, capture that too — keep going backwards!

End of Round

When no more captures are possible by either player, the round ends:

  • Remaining seeds on each side go to that player's store.
  • The player with fewer seeds refills holes right-to-left with 4 each.
  • The opponent fills the same number of opposite holes.

Winning

The game ends when a player cannot fill even one hole (fewer than 4 seeds left). Their opponent wins!
